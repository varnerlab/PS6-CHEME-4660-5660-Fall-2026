Problem Set 6 (PS6): Does a Synthetic Market Move Together Like the Real Market?

Release: Sunday, November 22, 2026
Initial submission deadline: Sunday, December 6, 2026, at 11:59 PM ET
Maximum score: 4 points

The L14a course example builds a synthetic market of SPY and 375 of its stocks from single-asset jump hidden Markov models: one model for SPY, one for each sector, and one for the stock-specific part of each stock. In PS6, we'll ask whether a synthetic market built this way moves together like the real market. You will fit the general factor model of lecture L14a and compare how the real and synthetic stocks move together. Standard fits the market and the sector factors. Advanced adds the Cholesky step, which keeps the correlations between the factors. Expert adds four economic factors from FRED, such as the daily change in the 10-year Treasury yield. You are graded on your calculations and interpretation, not on whether the synthetic market matches the real market.
