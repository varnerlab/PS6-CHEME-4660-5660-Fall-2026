Problem Set 6 (PS6): Do Hidden States Make the Farmer–Joshi Market Look Real?

Release: Sunday, November 22, 2026
Initial submission deadline: Sunday, December 6, 2026, at 11:59 PM ET
Maximum score: 4 points

The daily growth rates of real stock prices share a few stylized facts: fat tails, volatility clustering, and weak autocorrelation. In PS6 we'll ask whether hidden states, and random moves between them, help the Farmer–Joshi market from the L12a course example reproduce these facts for SPY. The market has value investors and trend followers, and each trader is short, flat, or long. You will compare the growth rates of the simulated market with the daily growth rates of SPY. Standard measures the course example's market. Advanced adds a conviction ladder and compares markets with 3, 5, and 7 states. Expert adds hesitant traders and compares three values of the probability that a trader moves. You are graded on correct calculations and interpretation, not on whether the market matches SPY.
