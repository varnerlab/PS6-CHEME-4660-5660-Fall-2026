# PS6 grading rubric

Your track is graded out of **4**:

| Score | What earns the score |
|:--:|:--|
| 0 | No check passes. |
| 1 | At least one check passes, but half or more of the checks fail. |
| 2 | More than half of the checks pass, but at least one check fails. |
| 3 | Every check passes, but the answers to the questions are not adequate. |
| 4 | Every check passes, and the answers to the questions are adequate. |

An adequate answer answers every part of the question and cites numbers from
`results/Report.md`. A 4 on Advanced also earns one **Magic Point**, and a 4 on
Expert earns two.
