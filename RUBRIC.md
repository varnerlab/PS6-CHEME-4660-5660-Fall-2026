# PS6 grading rubric

Your track is graded out of **4**. Standard has 12 checks, and Advanced has 24.
The checks grade your code, and the teaching team grades your answers.

| Score | What earns the score |
|:--:|:--|
| 0 | No check passes. |
| 1 | Standard: 1 to 6 checks pass. Advanced: 1 to 12 checks pass. |
| 2 | Standard: 7 to 11 checks pass. Advanced: 13 to 23 checks pass. |
| 3 | Every check passes, and at least one answer is incomplete. |
| 4 | Every check passes, and all three answers are complete. |

A complete answer answers every part of the question. The answers to Questions
2 and 3 must also cite numbers from `results/Report.md`. A 4 on Advanced also
earns one **Magic Point**.
